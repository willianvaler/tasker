# Pantry and shopping

The **Pantry** is a list type (**New** → List → **Pantry**) with household items: the **name**, **how much you have**, the **unit**, the **minimum** and how much to **buy each time**.

## From the pantry to the shopping list

The first time, choose the **shopping list**: **Create the Shopping list** creates a regular list in the same folder, or use another list already there. From then on:

- **Ran out** sets the item to zero; **−** and **+** change it by 1, and the pencil edits the numbers.
- An item that **ran out** or dropped **below the minimum** goes to the shopping list by itself, as "Rice (5 kg)" (the "buy each time"). It goes **only once**: lowering it more or changing the minimum does not repeat it.
- **Checking the purchase off** (on the shopping list, in Today or anywhere) **adds** the "buy each time" to the stock. If it is still below the minimum, the item goes back to the list. Undoing the purchase takes back what was added.
- If the stock goes back above the minimum without buying (you counted again), the item leaves the shopping list.
- Deleting the item removes the purchase that was still open.

Items you are short of come first, marked "On the shopping list".

## With the household

In a **project** folder, the pantry and the shopping list belong to everyone in the project: anyone who can create tasks changes the items, read-only members can see them. Whoever manages the project creates, deletes or changes the shopping list.

It works offline: the change stays on the device and, when it syncs, the server adds it to the shopping list. That way two phones that marked "ran out" at the same time do not create the purchase twice. There is no barcode scanning.
