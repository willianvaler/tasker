# Sets, reps and rest

Type `Bench press 4x12` and the task asks for **4 sets**. The row shows **0/4**: each tap counts a set, and the last one completes it (that's when XP comes). `x8` makes a counter: drink water 8 times.

## Rest

After each set a **rest** starts (60 s by default; change it under "How many times" in the panel). The countdown shows at the bottom, with **−30**, **+30** and **Skip**. On the phone you're notified even with the screen locked. In the browser, the notice comes if the tab is open, even hidden.

## Workout mode

On card pages or pages with tasks that have sets, **Start workout** opens full screen: current exercise, set, load, a big **Done** button (or `Space`), `R` skips the rest. After the last exercise, the workout closes by itself and a short notice shows sets, time, volume and XP. Works offline and keeps the screen on.

**Without unlocking the phone (Android):** with Workout Mode open, the set shows on the lock screen with **Done**, **Skip rest** and **+30 s**. Tap the notification's arrow to see the buttons. While the phone is locked, what you mark stays on the device and syncs when you open the app again.

## Mode: workout, focus or rest

The **Mode** button (sidebar; on the phone, **⚡ Mode** at the top of Today and Home; or `Ctrl+K`) gathers the three: **Workout** opens Workout Mode for a cards page or one with sets; **Focus** starts a pomodoro; **Rest** starts a 30 s to 5 min timer that alerts at the end with sound, vibration and a notification.

## Working out together

In a project workout, Workout mode asks **who is working out with you now**. Whoever you check gets a notice (in 🔔 and on the phone) and joins with **Join**. During the session, sets count for the group: anyone taps **Done** on whatever phone is at hand, and everyone sees the same count, the same exercise and the same rest. Whoever logs the last set completes the exercise and gets the XP, as with project tasks. The session ends when everyone leaves.

## Group challenges

In a shared project (or a team), **Group training** opens the challenges, the wall and the chat. **Start a new challenge** asks for the name and how many days it lasts; whoever works out on the most days by the end wins. A workout day counts when you **complete a workout page** or **spend 30 minutes or more in Workout mode** (one per day). On the **Wall**, check in today's workout with a photo if you like; the others react and comment. The **Chat** is for everything else.
