# Sets, reps and rest

Type `Bench press 4x12` and the task asks for **4 sets**. The row shows **0/4**: each tap counts a set, and the last one completes it (that's when XP comes). `x8` makes a counter: drink water 8 times.

## Rest

After each set a **rest** starts (60 s by default). In a workout, the exercise panel has **Sets · Reps · Weight · Rest** side by side: type the rest in seconds or minutes (`90`, `1:30`, `2min`). When an exercise ends, the rest also runs before the next one. The countdown shows at the bottom, with **−30**, **+30** and **Skip**. On the phone you're notified even with the screen locked. In the browser, the notice comes if the tab is open, even hidden.

**Advanced sets:** for a workout like "1 set of 14, 2 of 10, 1 of 8", turn on **Advanced sets** in the exercise panel. The first line stays the same, and the **+** at its end adds another line with its own sets, reps and weight (it starts with the reps and weight of the line before). The **×** removes a line. In Workout mode, each set shows what it asks for ("10 reps · 45kg"), and weight −/+ changes only that line's sets.

## Workout mode

On card pages or pages with tasks that have sets, **Start workout** opens full screen: current exercise, set, load, a big **Done** button (or `Space`), `R` skips the rest. After the last exercise, the workout closes by itself and a short notice shows sets, time, volume and XP. Works offline and keeps the screen on. Left for the list mid-workout? The button becomes **Resume workout** and you pick up where you stopped (in a training group, without asking again who's with you). Went back to a finished exercise with **Previous**? The big button becomes **Next exercise**. While the workout is paused, **Done** stays dimmed until you tap ▶.

**Without unlocking the phone (Android):** with Workout Mode open, the set shows on the lock screen with **Done**, **Skip rest** and **+30 s**. Tap the notification's arrow to see the buttons. While the phone is locked, what you mark stays on the device and syncs when you open the app again.

## Workout log

Every set done in Workout mode is saved as it happened: the weight, the reps you actually did (Workout mode starts with the plan; −1 and +1 change only this set) and the date. Cardio exercises ask for distance and time; timed ones, like the plank, keep the seconds. **Warm-up** logs a separate set that does not count toward the exercise total.

**History** shows in the exercise panel and in the Library page: the sessions from newest to oldest, with the sets and volume of each one. A wrong set goes away with the trash icon (only from the log; the workout stays as it is). The log is yours alone, even in a shared workout.

In Profile, under **Workout log**, pick **kg or lb** and, if you like, **effort per set**: RIR (reps you had left) or RPE (effort up to 10). Changing the scale does not change what was already logged; history shows it in the current scale.

## Records and 1RM

From your second workout of an exercise on, each set is compared with the earlier ones: heaviest weight, more reps at a weight you have done before, best **estimated 1RM** (Epley formula, only up to 12 reps), most volume in a day, longest time or longest distance. Workout mode tells you right away, the summary at the end counts the records and, with the game on, the hit of an exercise with a record is a **guaranteed critical**. Warm-ups never count.

The exercise panel and the Library page show the bests, which set the 1RM came from and how it changed day by day. The Library page also has the **1RM calculator**: weight and reps give the 1RM and the weights from 100% to 50%.

## Automatic progression

The weight for your next workout comes from your log. Each workout has a rule (in Configure page; the default is **double progression**, from 8 to 12 reps) and each exercise can have its own in the panel:

- **Linear**: did every rep of every set, go up one step;
- **Double**: at the same weight, go from 8 to 12 reps; hit 12 on every set, raise the weight and go back to 8;
- **Greyskull**: the last set goes to failure; beat the target, go up (twice the target doubles the step);
- **More time**: for timed exercises, held every set, 5 seconds more;
- **None**: the weight stays as it is.

The default step is 2.5 kg for the upper body and 5 kg for legs and back (5 and 10 lb); you can change it per exercise. A missing set or one with fewer reps is a failure, and a session with a failure never goes up; three failures in a row (one in Greyskull) bring a 10% **deload**. Exercises without weight progress in reps.

In a workout that is only yours, Workout mode opens the exercise with the new weight and says why ("Up 2.5 kg: you did 3×10 last time"). In a folder shared with others it only suggests, with **Use**, so it does not change everyone's plan. The exercise panel shows the next one and why.

## Weight and measurements

In **Weight and measurements** (in the Gym block of the sidebar) you log weight, body fat and measurements (waist, chest, hips, arm, thigh, calf, neck), with the date, and set a goal for each. The chart shows the goal as a dashed line and paints each stretch green when it got closer to the goal and red when it moved away. Weight uses the Profile unit; measurements are in centimeters.

Weight and measurements are health data, sensitive under Brazil’s LGPD: the first time, the app asks for your consent, and only then saves anything. They stay with you only (not even your coach sees them), are part of the export and leave with the account. **Stop saving and delete everything** withdraws consent and deletes the entries and goals right away.

## Workout stats

**Workout stats** (in the Gym block) shows your training days and time, the **muscle map** (front and back, male or female figure) shaded by the sets of each muscle in the week, the month or all time, with the list of muscles **not trained** in that period, **volume per week** (with average effort, if it is on) and the **training year**, one square per day toned by the time trained. The map only knows the muscles of items linked to a Library exercise: the target counts one set, each synergist half.

## Warm-up, plates and notes

Before the first set of a weighted exercise, Workout mode suggests a **warm-up** (40%, 60% and 80% of the weight, rounded to the step and never below the bar); each suggestion is one tap and goes to the log as a warm-up. For barbell exercises it shows the **plates per side** ("Per side: 2 × 20 + 2.5"). The bar and plates you have are in Profile, under Workout log; the exercise page has the **plate calculator**. The item notes show in Workout mode as a **note** ("bench on hole 4").

## Superset and circuit

In the exercise panel, **Superset or circuit** gives the item a letter (A, B, C, D). Items in the same workout with the same letter form a group: in Workout mode, a set of one goes straight to the next one in the group, with no rest, and rest comes at the end of each round. With three or more items it is a circuit. When one finishes, the others go on; when the whole group is done, the workout moves to the next exercise.

## Weekly plan

In **Configure page** of a workout, mark the **training days** (Monday to Sunday). The Today screen shows **Today’s workout**, with Open and **Reschedule** (tomorrow, the day after or skip), and the week in one line. Rescheduling is only yours and does not change the plan; the notice has **Undo**. At 6 pm, if the day’s workout has no set logged yet, a **reminder** arrives (turn it off in Profile, under Workout log).

## Gym in the game

Each completed exercise is a hit on the boss, like a task; with a **record** in the exercise, the hit is a **guaranteed critical**. Achievements have a **Gym** category: first record and 10, 50 and 100 records; 50, 100 and 365 training days; 10 and 100 tonnes lifted; **Full body** (chest, back, shoulders, arms, legs and abs as the target in the same week) and **Bench your bodyweight** (estimated bench press 1RM equal to or above your logged weight).

## Mode: workout, focus or rest

The **Mode** button (sidebar; on the phone, **⚡ Mode** at the top of Today and Home; or `Ctrl+K`) gathers the three: **Workout** opens Workout Mode for a cards page or one with sets; **Focus** starts a pomodoro; **Rest** starts a 30 s to 5 min timer that alerts at the end with sound, vibration and a notification.

## Working out together

To work out with other people, create a **training group**: in the Gym block (sidebar or Folders tab), tap **New training group**, name it and invite people with the link. The group shows up in the Gym block of whoever joins, and the workouts created in it belong to everyone. In a group workout (or a project one), Workout mode asks **who is working out with you now**. Whoever you check gets a notice (in 🔔 and on the phone) and joins with **Join**. During the session, sets count for the group: anyone taps **Done** on whatever phone is at hand, and everyone sees the same count, the same exercise and the same rest. Whoever logs the last set completes the exercise and gets the XP, as with project tasks. The session ends when everyone leaves.

## Group challenges

In a shared project (or a team), **Group training** opens the challenges, the wall and the chat. **Start a new challenge** asks for the name and how many days it lasts; whoever works out on the most days by the end wins. A workout day counts when you **complete a workout page** or **spend 30 minutes or more in Workout mode** (one per day). On the **Wall**, check in today's workout with a photo if you like; the others react and comment. The **Chat** is for everything else.
