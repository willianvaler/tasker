# Expenses and receipt photos

An **Expenses** page (New → Page → Expenses) is a spreadsheet of entries: what went out and what came in, with date, category, payment method and who paid. Use it for a renovation, a trip, an event or the house. It works offline, like tasks.

## Adding

- In the box at the top, type the **description** and the **amount** (`12.50`, `1,234.56`) and tap **Add** (or Enter). Pick **Expense** or **Income** and, if you want, a category.
- Tap a row to open the whole entry: date (`10/12`, `yesterday`), category, payment method, who paid (in a project), note. Closing saves. **Delete entry** sends it to the trash.
- At the top: **Spent**, **Income** and **Balance** for the period (**This month** or **All**), the **budget** with a bar of how much is used, and spending **by category**.
- In a project, Viewers see; Contributors add and change only their own entries; Editors, Managers and Owners change everything.

## Receipt photo

**Receipt photo** takes a picture (or picks several from the gallery) and reads the store, date, total, payment method and items. You check everything before saving: nothing goes in by itself. With **One entry per item**, each item on the receipt becomes a row. The photo is kept with the entry. A Brazilian e-receipt (NFC-e) with an access key isn't added twice: the app warns you if it's already there.

Reading needs internet and is done by Google (Gemini), which receives only the photo. While the app is in testing there is no per-account photo limit; if many are read in the same minute, the app asks you to wait a little.

## Photo of a list

When creating several tasks (**Several** on a page), **Photo of a list** reads a paper list (shopping, to-dos, workout) and puts each line in the text, with the usual quick syntax (`3x12 40kg`, `tomorrow`). Check the preview and create.

## Export

**Export** downloads what's on screen (the chosen period) as **CSV** (opens in Excel and Google Sheets) or **XLSX** (Excel, with currency values, dates and a summary sheet by category and month). On the phone, it opens Share.

## Split the bill

In a project with more than one person, every expense starts **split among all** members in equal shares. In the entry, **Split among** adds or removes people (with nobody selected, the expense stays out of the split); for it to count, also set **Paid by**. The **Split the bill** section shows how much each person gets back or owes, across all months, and the shortest way to settle up ("Bia pays $150.00 to Ana"). **Record settlement** adds that payment: it shows in the list with ⇄, does not count as an expense or income, and clears the debt. Wrong settlement: delete it and record it again.
