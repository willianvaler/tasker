# XP, streaks and bosses

Completing gives **XP**. XP raises your **level**. Doing something every day keeps your **streak**.

Every day a **daily boss** is born (about 6 tasks) and every week a **weekly boss** (about 5 days of use). Each task or habit is a hit that counts for both at once (and for the clan's). The daily one gives a smaller reward and has its own streak; the weekly one gives XP, gold and an item. The completion notice shows how much each has left.

## Fair, no tricks

- Creating and completing right away gives no XP (the hit still counts).
- Unchecking takes back what you earned.
- A parent task with subtasks gives no XP: the subtasks do.
- Pomodoro gives a small bonus, up to 4 a day.
- Each "good and bad" habit counts up to 10 "+" a day, and tasks created and completed within a few minutes have a daily cap. When a limit is reached, the notice says so.

## Achievements

There are 68, from **common** to **legendary** (the rarity name is written out, along with 1 to 5 dots). Profile → **See all achievements** lists them all, with filters by rarity and category, the % of players who have each one and how far you are from the next one in each track (tasks, streak, bosses, levels…). Some are **secret**: they only show a hint until you unlock them.

## Character

Pick a **class**, spend **attributes** and use one skill a day. Gold buys items in the **shop** (cosmetic only). The **inventory** (Profile or Character) shows everything you own, from the shop and from bosses, with filters by slot, rarity (common to legendary) and origin, and equips with one tap.

## Turning it off

Profile → **Gamification**. The app becomes just a task list.
