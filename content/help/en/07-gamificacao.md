# XP, streaks and bosses

Completing gives **XP**. XP raises your **level**. Doing something every day keeps your **streak**.

Every day a **daily boss** is born (about 6 tasks) and every week a **weekly boss** (about 5 days of use). Each task or habit is a hit that counts for both at once (and for the clan's). The daily one gives a smaller reward and has its own streak; the weekly one gives XP, gold and an item. The completion notice shows how much each has left.

## Three game levels

In **Settings → Features → Game** you choose: **Off** (just the lists; your history is kept), **Subtle** (the default: XP, character and bosses quietly beside the lists) or **RPG** (RPG: the **Game** screen becomes your **camp**, with your character up front, the daily and weekly bosses and today's open quests). The rules are the same in all three: switching never gains or loses anything.

## Fair, no tricks

- Creating and completing right away gives no XP (the hit still counts).
- Unchecking takes back what you earned.
- A parent task with subtasks gives no XP: the subtasks do.
- Pomodoro gives a small bonus, up to 4 a day.
- Each "good and bad" habit counts up to 10 "+" a day, and tasks created and completed within a few minutes have a daily cap. When a limit is reached, the notice says so.
- **Habit strength:** the small bar to the left of a "good and bad" habit changes color with use, from red (many "−" or neglected) to blue (many "+"). When the habit is weak, each "+" pulls the strength up faster (the XP is the same). In the habit panel you can make it **good only** or **bad only**: the other button goes away, and the strength halves each day it isn't used.

## Achievements

There are 68, from **common** to **legendary** (the rarity name is written out, along with 1 to 5 dots). More → Game → **Achievements** lists them all, with filters by rarity and category, the % of players who have each one and how far you are from the next one in each track (tasks, streak, bosses, levels…). Some are **secret**: they only show a hint until you unlock them.

## Character

Pick a **class** (your character gets its painted portrait; change the portrait and the scene in **Character → Appearance**), spend **attributes** and use one skill a day. The damage math is under **How damage works**, in Character. Gold buys items in the **shop** (cosmetic only). The **inventory** (Game or Character) shows everything you own, from the shop and from bosses, with filters by slot, rarity (common to legendary) and origin, and equips with one tap.

## Rebirth

From **level 20**, the **Rebirth** panel on the Character screen takes you back to level 1. The daily and weekly bosses go back to the starting difficulty from the next period. You keep your gold, items, pets, achievements and attributes. Each rebirth gives **+5% XP forever**, up to +25%, and has its own achievements. To confirm, you type "rebirth".

## Turning it off

Settings → Features → **Game** → **Off**. The app becomes just a task list.
