# Meetings

A **meeting** is a kind of list: create it with **New list → Meeting** in a folder or project (or through the project's **Find a time**, which brings the date, time and people). Whoever creates it is already a participant.

## Before: date, place and agenda

At the top, the **date** (`tomorrow`, `11/24`), the **time**, the **duration**, the **place or call link**, the **participants** (the project's people) and **outside guests** (names only). **Repeat** (every week or every 2 weeks) sets the next date. Tap **Save date and place**.

The **agenda** is the items, each with planned minutes and, optionally, a **presenter**. As you type, project tasks with that name show up: tapping one **links the item to the task**, and the agenda shows its state (open, in progress, done). Tapping an item opens its panel, like a task, to change the name, time or presenter.

The meeting counts as **busy** for its participants: the booking page and **Find a time** don't offer that time.

## During: running it

**Start meeting** highlights the first item and starts a **timer** with its time. The current item has a **notes** field; **Next item** moves on (with its own timer). Below are the **general notes** and who is **present** (all checked; uncheck who missed it).

To record an **action item**, write a line starting with `[ ]`, like quick add: `[ ] send the proposal @Ana friday !!`.

## After: minutes and action items

**End meeting** does three things:

- Each `[ ]` line becomes a **real task**, in the folder's first list (or the Inbox), with the `@name` as assignee and dates and priority as in quick add. The task's description links back to the meeting.
- The agenda, notes, attendance and action items become the **minutes**: a note in the folder, part of search, links and version history. **Open the minutes** takes you there.
- The meeting becomes read-only.

**Schedule the next one** creates the next meeting in the series, with the same agenda (without notes), the same people and the date from the repeat. It starts with **Action items from the last meeting**, each with its current state.

## Who can

Creating, editing the agenda, running and ending needs the **Create and run meetings** permission (Owner, Manager and Editor; it also shows in custom roles). Viewers see the meeting and the minutes and comment on items.
