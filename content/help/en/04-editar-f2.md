# Quick edit with F2

Select a task (click it or use `J` and `K`) and press **F2**: the title becomes a field. Enter saves, Esc cancels.

With no task selected, **F2** renames the open page or folder, and you can change its **icon** right there.

## Other list shortcuts

- `Space` or `X` — complete
- `1`, `2`, `3` — priority
- `D` — date · `Z` — snooze to tomorrow
- `M` — add to My day
- `S` — start · `P` — pomodoro · `Shift+T` — stopwatch
- `Delete` — delete (with Undo)
- `Ctrl+Z` — undo · `Shift+Z` — redo what was undone
- `→` and `←` — expand and collapse subtasks

The full list is under **Shortcuts** below, or press `?`.

## Change a shortcut

On a computer, in the **Shortcuts** table, click the pencil next to a shortcut and press the new key (Esc cancels). If another shortcut in the same area already uses that key, the app tells you and keeps the old one. The change applies right away and stays on this device; the circular arrow restores the default key.
