# Community

The **Community** (sidebar, `Ctrl+K` or the globe at the top of Folders) has projects, workouts, menus, lists and habit routines people have published. Search by name, filter by type and see the **most used** or the **newest**.

## Use

Open a post to see its lists and items. **Use** creates a **folder of yours** with the copy: change, delete and share it as you like, without touching the original. **Like** helps the good ones show up first.

## Publish

In the menu of a **folder** (or project) or a **saved template**, or under **Share** on a **list**, tap **Publish to the community**, give it a title, a short description and the type. A **copy of the structure** goes: lists, tasks, subtasks, sets, recurrence and notes. **Not included:** people, assignees, dates, comments, attachments or expenses. Whoever publishes shows up by **nick**. In **My posts** you see yours and can **Unpublish** (whoever already used it keeps their copy).

## Report

Saw something that should not be there? **Report** it, with a reason if you like. After a few reports from different people, the post leaves the list until it is reviewed.
