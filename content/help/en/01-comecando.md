# Getting started in 1 minute

Type in the **New task** field and press Enter. Done: the task goes to the Inbox (or to the open page).

## Quick syntax

The text itself fills in the details:

- `tomorrow`, `friday`, `10/15` — due date
- `!`, `!!`, `!!!` — priority
- `#groceries` — label
- `/daily`, `/weekly`, `/mon,wed` — repeat
- `3x12` — 3 sets of 12; `x8` — do it 8 times
- `@ana` — assignee (in a project)

Example: `Pay the power bill friday !! #home` becomes "Pay the power bill", due Friday, medium priority, label home.

## Many at once

Paste a list (one per line) or tap **Many**. Indented lines become subtasks. You see a preview before creating.

## Checking off

Tap the box. Mistake? The notice has **Undo**.
