# Getting started in 1 minute

Type in the **New task** field and press Enter. Done: the task goes to the Inbox (or to the open list).

## Quick syntax

The text itself fills in the details:

- `tomorrow`, `friday`, `10/15` — due date
- `3pm`, `at 9:30`, `14:05` — time (no date means today); it reminds you at that time, and you can change the reminder in the panel
- `!`, `!!`, `!!!` — priority
- `#groceries` — label
- `/daily`, `/weekly`, `/mon,wed` — repeat
- `3x12` — 3 sets of 12; `x8` — do it 8 times
- `@ana` — assignee (in a project)

Example: `Pay the power bill friday !! #home` becomes "Pay the power bill", due Friday, medium priority, label home.

## Many at once

Paste a list (one per line), tap **Many** or use **+ → Several at once**. Indented lines become subtasks. You see a preview before creating.

## From another app

On a link, a message or a note in another app, tap **Share** and pick **Questlist**. **Received from another app** opens: **Create a task** (the title comes filled in and the text and link go to the description; you pick the list) or, when there are several lines, **one task per line** in the Inbox, checking first. On Android, it works with the app installed from the APK (from this version on); on a computer or a phone without the APK, install the app from the browser (Chrome or Edge: **Install app** in the menu) and it shows up in Share. On iPhone, not yet. **Photo or PDF** (from the gallery, email, WhatsApp): **New task with the attachment** creates the task in the list you pick, with the file name as the title and the file as the attachment. Large photos are reduced; PDFs go up to 10 MB (above that, the screen explains and does not create it). Images and PDFs through Share only work in the Android app (needs the new APK).

## Checking off

Tap the box. Mistake? The notice has **Undo**.

## Where things are

- **Today**: what to do now (overdue, due today, today's habits). It's the opening screen.
- **Plan**: calendar, weekly review, the next 7 days, the Inbox without a date and what was assigned to you.
- **Folders**: folders, lists and projects (and the Gym, if you work out).
- **+** in the middle of the bottom bar (or **New** in the sidebar): creates a task, habit, note, list, folder or project.
- **More**: everything else, each with a name and a line explaining it: Habits, Wellness, People (friends, teams, clan), Game, Settings, Help, Trash. On the computer, the card with your name at the bottom of the sidebar opens More.

On the phone, the two spots beside the **+** are yours (at first, **Notes** and **Folders**): change them in **Settings → Navigation** (Notes, Plan, Inbox, Habits, Folders, Workouts, Game or People). Whatever isn't on the bar is under **More**. The **magnifier** at the top finds any list, folder, screen or action.
