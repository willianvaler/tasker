# Frequently asked questions

**I deleted something by mistake.** Use Undo on the notice. After that, Profile → **Trash** keeps it for 30 days.

**My task disappeared from Today.** It was completed, snoozed or its date changed. Find it with `Ctrl+K`.

**Why didn't I get XP?** Completed right after creating, unchecked, or it's a parent task. See "XP, streaks and bosses".

**The rest alert didn't ring on my computer.** The browser only alerts with the tab open (it can be hidden) and notification permission granted.

**How do I export my data?** Profile → Privacy → Export (JSON or CSV).

**How do I leave a project?** In the project, **People** → **Leave project**.

**Can I use it without the game part?** Yes: Profile → Gamification.

**Where do I change the language?** Profile → Language.
