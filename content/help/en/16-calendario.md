# Calendar and Google Calendar

The **Calendar** (in **Plan**, in the sidebar or with `G C`) shows the tasks with a date from all your folders and projects, by **month** or by **week**. Tap a day to see its list; tap a task to open it.

- A repeating task shows up on every day it will be due.
- A **workout** (a list in the Gym folder or in a workout group) shows up once per day, with the list name and how many exercises fall on it. Tapping it opens the list.
- Habits stay on the Habits screen, not in the calendar.

## In Google Calendar

Below the calendar, tap **Create calendar link** and then **Add to Google Calendar**. Or copy the link and, in Google Calendar on a computer, go to **Other calendars → + → From URL** and paste it. It also works in Apple Calendar and Outlook ("subscribe to calendar").

- Tasks with a date show up there (completed ones with a ✓ in front), and each workout as an all-day event, with the exercises and the list link.
- It only goes one way: changing the event in Google does not change the task.
- Google updates it on its own, but a change (or the first batch of tasks) may take from a few hours to a day to show. Tasks without a date don't show up: give them a date.
- Anyone with the link can see your task titles. If it leaks, use **Change link** (the old one stops working) or **Turn off**.
