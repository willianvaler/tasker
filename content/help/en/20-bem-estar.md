# Wellness and medicines

**Wellness** brings health together: your workouts, diet, healthy habits and medicines. It is in the sidebar, in **More**, and it can take one of the bottom bar positions (Settings → Navigation).

## Gym inside Wellness

In **Settings → Features**, with Workouts on, the **Gym inside Wellness** switch puts workouts, groups, the Library, Weight and measurements and Stats inside the Wellness screen. The Gym then leaves the sidebar block, the Folders screen and the bottom bar options (if Workouts was on the bar, you now see Wellness). Nothing moves or gets lost: turn the switch off and everything goes back.

## Diet: weekly menu

In **Wellness → Diet**, **Plan the week's menu** creates the **Diet** folder with the **Weekly menu** list and opens the grid: one card per day, with **Breakfast**, **Lunch**, **Snack** and **Dinner**. A meal's **+** adds a dish: the name, the meal, **on which days** (one or several) and the **ingredients**, one per line (`200 g chicken`). Tapping a dish opens the task, to edit or delete it; **View as a list** opens the regular list.

In Wellness, **Today's menu** shows the day's dishes to check off (checking completes the ingredients too). A checked dish stays checked until the week turns, on Monday, and a dish you didn't eat never becomes overdue. **Make a shopping list** gathers the week's ingredients, adding up quantities ("Chicken (400 g)"), and opens batch creation so you can review it and pick the destination list.

## Healthy living

Ready-made habits to add to the **Habits** screen with one tap: drink water (8 glasses a day, with a counter), sleep 8 hours, walk, 10,000 steps, eat fruit (2 a day), vegetables, skip sugar, stretch, meditate, no screens before bed and get some sun. The ones already in your habits show as **Added**. Once added, it's a habit like any other: you can change the name, goal and days. **Weight and measurements** is here too.

## Medicines

The first time, the app asks for your consent: medicines are health data, sensitive personal data under the LGPD. They stay with you only, are included in your data export and are deleted with your account. **Stop storing medicines** (at the end of the section) deletes them all.

- **New medicine:** the name, **pills per dose** (half works: `0.5`), **how many times a day** (1×, 2×, 3×, 4× or 6×), **which days** (every day, every other day, or once a week), **pills per box** and **how many you have now** (empty means one box).
- Each medicine shows how many are **left** today and **how long they last**. With 7 days or fewer left, it says **buy soon**; after the last day, **Ran out**.
- **Bought:** enter how many pills you bought (the box size is filled in); they are added to what is left.
- In the medicine’s **…**: **Edit**, **Count again** (count what is left and the estimate restarts today), **Stop taking** (no more counting down until you take it again) and **Delete**.

The estimate assumes you took every dose since the last count, starting the day after it. If you missed some, use **Count again**. Medicines work offline, like tasks; giving consent needs a connection.
