# Working with other people

In a project, open **People** and tap **Create invite link**. Send the link: whoever opens it creates an account right there and joins.

Before inviting, you can write `@gregory` in tasks. When Gregory joins, he says "I'm @gregory" and those tasks become his.

## Roles

- **Owner** — everything, including deleting the project
- **Manager** — everything but deleting the project
- **Editor** — works on everything, doesn't manage people
- **Contributor** — creates and does their own tasks
- **Viewer** — only sees and comments

Teams can have **custom roles**, picking permission by permission.

## I'll take it

On a task with nobody assigned, **I'll take it** makes you the assignee. Great for splitting the barbecue.
