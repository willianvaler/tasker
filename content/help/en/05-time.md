# Working with other people

In a project, open **People** and tap **Create invite link**. Send the link: whoever opens it creates an account right there and joins.

Before inviting, you can write `@gregory` in tasks. When Gregory joins, he says "I'm @gregory" and those tasks become his.

## Roles

- **Owner** — everything, including deleting the project
- **Manager** — everything but deleting the project
- **Editor** — works on everything, doesn't manage people
- **Contributor** — creates and does their own tasks
- **Viewer** — only sees and comments

Teams can have **custom roles**, picking permission by permission.

## I'll take it

On a task with nobody assigned, **I'll take it** makes you the assignee. Great for splitting the barbecue.

## Reactions

In a project you can react with 👏 🔥 💪 ❤️ or 🎉 to a completed task (in the project's **Activity** or in the task's comments) and to each comment. Tap the face with the **+** to pick one; tap the emoji again to remove it. Whoever completed it (or wrote the comment) gets a notification. Reacting needs internet, and anyone who can comment can react, Viewers included.

## Friends

**Friends** (More → People, or `Ctrl+K`) holds your **nick**, the name people find you by (3 to 20 lowercase letters, numbers, `_` or `.`; you can change it). To add someone, type their **@nick** or **e-mail** and tap **Send request**: they get a notification and accept or decline. By e-mail, the app does not say whether the account exists. Your **friend link** is the fastest way: whoever opens it and signs in becomes your friend right away (they can create the account right there). If the link ends up where it should not, **Change link** and the old one stops working. Unfriending does not notify the other person.

**Add from friends:** in a project's **People** (and a workout group's), on the team list and in the clan, the **Add from friends** list shows who is not there yet. **Add** puts the person straight into the group (in a project, with the "Invite as" role), and they get a notification; if they do not want it, they can just leave. It only shows for whoever can already invite in that group.

**Who sees each task:** in a folder of yours (not a project), the task panel has **Who sees it**: **Private** (the default: only you), **Friends** or **Public** (anyone with an account). In quick capture, `+friends` or `+public`. Tasks that are not private show on your **profile** (Friends → Profile), with only the title, date, status and list name: notes, attachments and comments never leave. The row shows 👥 (friends) or 🌐 (public). In a project, whoever is in the project sees it, and the marker does not show.
