# Sending a list (a workout for a student)

Build the list (a workout, a meal plan, homework) and tap **Share → Send to…** (at the top of the list).

- **Live** — you keep adjusting; the recipient sees the changes and tracks their own progress. Each student only sees their own.
- **Copy** — the person gets a copy that becomes theirs.

Send it by **link** (WhatsApp, for example) or **email**. People without an account create one right there and land on the list.

## The recipient

The list shows up under **Received**. They can check things off, log sets and load, leave a **note** ("my shoulder hurt") and use Workout mode, offline too.

## Students panel

On a sent list, **Share → Students** shows each one: how much they did this week, adherence over the last 4 weeks, how their loads evolved and their notes. You can **remind** whoever went quiet and **archive** whoever stopped.

Under **Training**, the panel reads the sets the student did in this list's exercises: records, volume over the last 28 days, the best estimated 1RM of each exercise (and which set it came from) and, if the workout has days in the weekly plan, how many planned days they hit (training on another day of the same week counts). A **stalled** exercise is one that went 3 sessions without beating the earlier best; it shows first, and the student's row says how many are stalled. The rest of the student's log stays theirs.

Under **This student's plan**, you pick, exercise by exercise, the progression rule and starting load that apply only to that student (empty follows the list). The starting load applies until their first workout (with the **None** rule, always); after that, the rule follows the student's log. In Workout mode, the student sees their own load and "Set by your coach".
