# Game mode

In **Settings → Features → Game → RPG**, Questlist becomes a medieval fantasy game, like Habitica: the same tasks, with more at stake.

## Camp

**Home** shows your painted character (pick the portrait and scene in **Character → Appearance**), **health**, **mana**, the daily and weekly bosses and the quests in progress. The vocabulary changes: tasks become **quests**, and the habits tab becomes **Habits & dailies** (you can turn this off in Settings).

## Health and dailies

Dailies are recurring tasks and check habits in your own folders (project ones don't count). The first time you open the app each day, it asks **"What did you do yesterday?"**: check what you did and just forgot to record. Every daily left behind costs health (more for high priority; Constitution reduces it). Leveling up refills health; a **potion** (25 gold) gives back 15.

**Tavern:** traveling or sick? Rest at the tavern and dailies won't cost health.

**Perfect day:** completing every daily gives a bonus to all attributes the next day.

## Death

If health hits zero, your character falls. You choose: **buy a life for 500 gold** (keep everything) or **start over at level 1**, losing level, XP, gold, items, attributes and pets. Achievements stay.

## Mana and skills

Every completed task gives mana, and the start of the day refills more. Each class has 4 skills, unlocked at levels 3, 5, 8 and 11: strikes on bosses, healing, shield (the next day start costs only half), stealth (avoids missed dailies) and gold.

## Rewards and armoire

In **Rewards** you create real-life treats with a gold price ("one episode: 30") and redeem them whenever you want. This works in Subtle mode too. The **enchanted armoire** (100 gold) gives you a shop item you don't have yet.

## Pets, mounts and quests

Completing tasks drops **eggs**, **hatching potions**, **food** and sometimes **scrolls**. In the **Stable**: egg + potion = pet; feed it (its favorite food counts more) until it becomes a **mount**. Your pet and mount appear with your character. A scroll starts a **quest**: a boss with a story that takes a hit for every task, solo or with your clan, within 14 days. You can also buy scrolls with gold.

## Gear

Battle gear gives attributes by rarity. Turn on the **costume** to change only the look.
