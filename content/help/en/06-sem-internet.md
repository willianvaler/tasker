# Works offline

After the first sync, the app keeps your data **on the device**. With no signal you keep creating, checking, editing and working out. When the connection returns, everything goes up on its own, in the order you did it.

## The sync dot

- **Green** — all caught up
- **Yellow** — sending or receiving
- **Red** — offline (what you do is kept) or something was refused; open it to see which

Tap it (or Profile → Sync) to see the queue and force a send.

## Conflicts

If you and someone else change the same task offline, the last change **for each field** wins. For the description, the losing version is kept: "See previous version" shows up.

## What needs a connection

Invites, the students panel, automations, reports and the shop.
