# Pomodoro and time

**Pomodoro:** 25 minutes of focus, a 5-minute break, and a long break every 4 focus rounds. Start it in the task panel, with `P` on the selected task or from the palette (`Ctrl+K`).

When focus ends you get a break idea: grab a coffee, get some air, stretch. Change the ideas, their order and the durations in **Profile → Pomodoro**.

## Logging time

- The task **stopwatch**: in the panel or `Shift+T`.
- **Log by hand**: type `45m`, `1h30 yesterday` or `30 10/14`.
- Each finished pomodoro counts too.

The panel shows "Done: 1h20 of 2h" (against the estimate) and the parent adds up its subtasks. In **Profile → Pomodoro** you see today, the week and where the time went; in the project, time per list and per person.
