# Planning the day and automating

- **My day** — pick what you'll do today (`M` or in the panel), put it in order and say how much time you have: the app shows whether it fits.
- **Do now** — the suggestion of what to do next: what's already started, your day, what's overdue.
- **Snooze** — the alarm clock on the row (or `Z`) moves it to tomorrow.
- **Fields** — in the page menu, create fields like Cost, Stage or Buyer.
- **Approval** — a task only one person (or a manager) can complete: the owner approves the budget before buying.

## Automations

In a project, **Automations** creates "when… then…" rules. Example: when a task in the On the day list is completed, create "Clean the grill" in the After list. Up to 20 per project; if an action can't run, it shows in the recent runs and nothing is lost.

## Eisenhower matrix

On a list page, the **Matrix** tab splits the open tasks into four quadrants: **Do** (important and urgent), **Schedule** (important, no rush), **Delegate** (urgent, not very important) and **Drop** (neither). Important means priority `!!` or `!!!`; urgent means due by the day after tomorrow, overdue included. The button next to a task moves it to another quadrant: it adjusts the priority and, when needed, the date (due today, or a week from now). The page remembers the last tab used on this device.

## Weekly review

Once a week, Today suggests the **Weekly review**; it always lives in **Plan** (and in search, `Ctrl+K`). It has four steps: **Done** (what you did in the last 7 days, by page), **Left over** (overdue tasks: pick a new day, remove the date or complete them), **Stalled** (no date and untouched for more than 30 days: give a date or archive, which sends it to the trash) and **Next week** (what is due in the next 7 days). **Finish review** saves the date; "Not now" hides the prompt for a week.
